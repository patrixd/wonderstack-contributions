# Licence of the training descriptions (`training/`)

The files in `training/` — the front matter and the description written by the contributor — are licensed under the
**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International** licence (CC BY-SA 4.0). The full legal code is at
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode>.

The course, video or guide a file **links to** is not covered by this licence: it keeps its own licence, stated in the
file's `licence:` field (`free-to-access` means "free to use, all rights reserved by its author"; we only link to it).
